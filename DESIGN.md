# DESIGN.md — Marcelo Goncalves Visual World

## Mode
Persuade. Visitors decide to book. Design should feel calm, confident, and clinically precise.

## Visual authority
No incumbent; this brief replaces the earlier generic personal brand world.

## Pinned world
- **Era / feel:** Contemporary wellness + clinical precision. Quiet luxury, not corporate.
- **Materials:** Satin ceramic, soft paper, brushed metal. Minimal texture, maximum clarity.
- **Fonts:** One refined sans for display + one warm sans for body.

## Palette
- Background: very light warm gray `#F6F4F1`
- Foreground: deep charcoal `#161816`
- Accent: sage green `#6B8A6E`
- Muted: warm gray `#7C766D`
- Surface: white `#FFFFFF`
- Alert: soft red `#C25B52`

## Motion
- Slow fades on scroll
- Gentle lift on cards/links
- Nothing startling

## Spacing
- 8px scale
- Generous whitespace; frames everything carefully

## Copy style
Clear, direct, warm. Active voice. Short paragraphs.
